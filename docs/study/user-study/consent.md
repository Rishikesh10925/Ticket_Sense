# Consent form: TicketSense user study

**Project.** TicketSense, a final-year capstone at NMIMS MPSTME Hyderabad by Rishikesh,
Aashritha Reddy and Shivaganesh. The system drafts replies to IT and HR support tickets
and decides when a draft can be trusted.

**What you will do.** Handle two sets of 20 support tickets on a computer, one set with
AI-drafted replies and one without, then answer a ten-question usability survey and two
short questions. It takes about 75 minutes.

**What is recorded.** How long each ticket takes, the reply you choose or write, your
survey answers and your comments. You are recorded under a code (for example P03), never
your name.

**What is not recorded.** No audio, video or screen recording, and no personal data
beyond your consent.

**Data.** Every ticket is synthetic: no real company, customer or person appears in it.
Results are reported only as group summaries in the project report.

**Your rights.** Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time, without giving a
reason, and ask for your data to be deleted until the report is submitted.
