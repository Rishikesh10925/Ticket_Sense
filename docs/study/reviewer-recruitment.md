# Outside reviewer recruitment

Owner: Aashritha. Blueprint V2 §11.6; Roadmap Weeks 1, 3, 5 and 6; risk R1.

Target: **eight confirmed** reviewers by the end of Week 5 (at least six must stay
active; four is the floor in the cut list). Reviewers label about 100 drafts each, plus
about 25 from a shared overlap set.

## Who qualifies

- Not an author of TicketSense (not R, A or S).
- Comfortable reading short IT-support text: classmates, juniors, or friends in CSE,
  IT or data-science courses.
- Can commit about 4 hours in total across Weeks 7 and 8, plus one 45-minute
  training session in Week 6.

>
> We're building TicketSense, our final-year capstone at NMIMS MPSTME Hyderabad: a system
> that drafts replies to IT support tickets and decides when a draft can be trusted.
>
> We need a few people to judge whether AI-written replies are good enough to send.
> You'd read a short ticket, the drafted reply and the documents it cites, then pick one
> of four labels. It takes about two minutes per draft.
>
> **What it involves**
> - One 45-minute online training session in the week of 23 November.
