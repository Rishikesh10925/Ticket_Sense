# Health Insurance Claim Process

**Department:** HR

## Issue
Employee needs to file a health insurance claim or check on the status of
one already submitted.

## Resolution
1. Claims for in-network providers are typically filed directly by the
   provider — the employee usually doesn't need to submit anything
   themselves in that case.
2. For out-of-network or reimbursement-style claims, the employee submits
   through the insurance provider's portal (not the HR portal) with an
   itemized bill and proof of payment.
3. HR can help clarify plan coverage questions but does not process
   individual claims directly — those go through the insurance carrier.
4. For a claim stuck in processing beyond the carrier's stated turnaround
   time, HR can assist by escalating to the carrier's dedicated employer
   support line, which the employee doesn't have direct access to.

## Notes
Never ask an employee to share detailed medical information in a ticket —
claim status questions should be handled by claim number/reference only.
