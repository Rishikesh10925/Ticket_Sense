# How to Apply for Leave

**Department:** HR

## Issue
Employee needs to know the process for requesting time off.

## Resolution
1. Submit the leave request through the HR portal, specifying leave type and
   dates.
2. The request routes automatically to the employee's direct manager for
   approval.
3. Leave should be requested at least 2 weeks in advance where possible;
   sick leave can be logged after the fact but should still be submitted
   within 3 working days of returning.
4. Approved leave appears on the team calendar automatically; the employee
   will get a confirmation notification once approved.

## Notes
If a manager hasn't responded to a leave request within 5 working days, the
employee should follow up directly rather than assuming approval.
