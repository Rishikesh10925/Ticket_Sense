# Expense Reimbursement Process

**Department:** HR

## Issue
Employee needs to submit a business expense for reimbursement or is asking
about the status of a submitted claim.

## Resolution
1. Expenses are submitted through the expense portal with an itemized
   receipt attached — claims without a receipt above the minor-expense
   threshold will be rejected.
2. Manager approval is required before the claim moves to payment
   processing.
3. Approved claims are typically reimbursed in the next payroll cycle after
   approval, not immediately.
4. For claims flagged as needing more information, respond directly in the
   portal rather than resubmitting a new claim, to avoid duplicates.

## Notes
Expense category limits (e.g., meal per-diem caps) are set by policy and
are not something the helpdesk can override — direct policy exception
questions to the employee's manager.
