# New Hire Onboarding Checklist

**Department:** HR

## Issue
Manager or new hire needs to know what happens before/on the first day.

## Resolution
Before day one:
- IT provisions accounts, laptop, and system access based on the role
  profile submitted by the manager.
- HR sends the new hire pre-start paperwork (contract, tax forms, banking
  details) to complete before day one.

On day one:
- Building/badge access is issued at reception with photo ID.
- New hire completes mandatory compliance training within the first week.
- Manager conducts a first-day orientation covering team structure and
  immediate priorities.

## Notes
Delays in day-one readiness are almost always caused by the role profile/
access request being submitted late — this should go in as soon as the
offer is signed, not the week before start date.
