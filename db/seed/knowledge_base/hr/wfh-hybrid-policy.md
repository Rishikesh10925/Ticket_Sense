# Work-From-Home / Hybrid Policy

**Department:** HR

## Issue
Employee wants clarification on remote work expectations or requests an
exception to the standard policy.

## Resolution
1. Standard policy is role-dependent — confirm the employee's role category
   (office-based, hybrid, fully remote) as defined in their employment
   terms, since expectations differ by category.
2. Hybrid roles typically have a minimum required number of in-office days
   per week, set by the department, not individually negotiable by default.
3. Exception requests (e.g., temporary full remote for a personal
   circumstance) go through the employee's manager with HR notified, not
   directly to HR as a self-service request.
4. Equipment for remote work (monitor, chair, etc.) requests are handled
   separately through the standard equipment request process.

## Notes
Policy exceptions are manager decisions within HR guardrails — HR's role
here is policy clarification, not approval authority over individual
arrangements.
