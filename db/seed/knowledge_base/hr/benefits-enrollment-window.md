# Benefits Enrollment Window

**Department:** HR

## Issue
Employee wants to enroll in or change benefits (health insurance,
retirement contributions, etc.) outside of when they expected to be able to.

## Resolution
1. Benefits enrollment is generally only open during the annual open
   enrollment window or within 30 days of a qualifying life event (marriage,
   birth, loss of other coverage).
2. New hires get an initial enrollment window starting from their start
   date — confirm this hasn't already lapsed before troubleshooting further.
3. For a qualifying life event outside open enrollment, the employee must
   submit supporting documentation (e.g., marriage certificate, birth
   certificate) within the 30-day window.
4. Requests outside these windows without a qualifying event cannot be
   processed until the next open enrollment period.

## Notes
Always confirm the specific qualifying event date — the 30-day window is
calculated from the event, not from when the employee remembers to submit
the request.
