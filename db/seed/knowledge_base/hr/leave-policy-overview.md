# Leave Policy Overview

**Department:** HR

## Issue
Employee wants a summary of leave types and annual entitlement.

## Resolution
Standard leave categories:
- **Annual/Vacation leave** — accrued monthly, carries over up to a capped
  number of days into the next year.
- **Sick leave** — available from day one of employment; a doctor's note is
  required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.
- **Casual leave** — short-notice personal leave, limited days per year.
- **Bereavement, parental, and jury duty leave** — governed by separate
  policies with their own entitlement and documentation rules.

Exact entitlement days depend on location and employment type — direct
employees to the HR portal's leave policy page for their specific region,
since entitlements vary by country.

## Notes
This article covers general categories only; region-specific entitlement
tables are maintained separately and change more frequently.
