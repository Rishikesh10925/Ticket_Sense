# Timesheet Submission Deadline and Process

**Department:** HR

## Issue
Employee or manager has a question about timesheet deadlines or a missed
submission.

## Resolution
1. Timesheets are due weekly, by end of day Friday, submitted through the
   HR portal.
2. Manager approval is required before the timesheet counts toward payroll
   processing — employees should submit early enough to allow for approval
   before the payroll cutoff.
3. A missed weekly submission can usually still be filed late for the same
   pay period up until the payroll cutoff; after that, it rolls into the
   following pay cycle.
4. Repeated late submissions from the same employee/team should be flagged
   to the manager rather than repeatedly handled as one-off exceptions.

## Notes
Contractors and hourly staff have a stricter cutoff than salaried staff —
confirm employment type before quoting a deadline.
