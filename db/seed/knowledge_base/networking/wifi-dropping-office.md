# Wi-Fi Repeatedly Dropping in Office

**Department:** Networking

## Issue
Laptop repeatedly disconnects from the office Wi-Fi network, especially when
moving between areas or after periods of inactivity.

## Resolution
1. Confirm which SSID and access point the device last connected to
   (Settings → Wi-Fi → connection details) — this helps identify whether
   it's one access point or building-wide.
2. Forget and reconnect to the network to clear a stale saved profile.
3. Disable Wi-Fi power-saving mode in the adapter's advanced settings —
   this is the most common cause of drops after idle periods.
4. If the drops happen consistently in one specific area of the building,
   report the location — it likely indicates an access point needing a
   restart or has a coverage gap.

## Notes
Drops affecting many users in the same area at the same time indicate an
access point or controller issue, not a per-device problem.
