## Symptom
An employee wants to add a spouse, partner or child to their health insurance.

## Cause
Dependants can be added only within 30 days of becoming eligible (marriage, birth,
adoption) or during open enrolment.

## Resolution steps
1. Confirm the relationship and the date of the event that made them eligible.
2. Within 30 days of the event, the employee adds the dependant in the HR portal under
   Benefits and uploads proof of relationship.
3. Coverage starts from the event date once approved.
4. Outside the window, the dependant can be added at the next open enrolment.

## Warnings
The premium deduction for the dependant starts on the next payslip and may include
arrears back to the coverage start date.

## Key facts
- Dependants are added within 30 days of becoming eligible, or at open enrolment.
- Proof of relationship is uploaded in the HR portal.
- Coverage for a newly added dependant starts from the event date.
