# Payroll Dates and Payslip Access

**Department:** HR

## Issue
Employee has a question about when they'll be paid or can't find their
payslip.

## Resolution
1. Payroll runs monthly, with salary credited on the last working day of the
   month; confirm the exact cutoff date hasn't shifted for the current month
   due to a holiday.
2. Payslips are available in the HR portal under Payroll → Payslips,
   typically published 2 business days before pay date.
3. If a payslip is missing for a month the employee was paid, check whether
   they were on a payroll run exception that month (e.g., mid-month starter/
   leaver) before escalating to payroll.
4. Discrepancies in the actual amount paid (not just access issues) should
   be routed to payroll directly with the specific line item in question.

## Notes
Do not disclose payslip or salary details to anyone other than the employee
themselves without written manager/HR authorization.
