## Symptom
An employee going on a business trip asks for money in advance to cover expenses.

## Cause
Travel advances are allowed for trips longer than three days when no corporate card is
held.

## Resolution steps
1. Request the advance in the expense portal at least five working days before travel.
2. The manager approves it.
3. After the trip, the employee settles the advance by submitting the expense claim within
   ten working days of returning.
4. Any unspent amount is deducted from the next salary.

## Warnings
A new advance cannot be requested while a previous one is unsettled.

## Key facts
- Travel advances are for trips longer than three days without a corporate card.
- Advances are requested at least five working days before travel.
- The advance is settled with an expense claim within ten working days of returning.
