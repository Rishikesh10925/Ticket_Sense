# Resignation and Notice Period Policy

**Department:** HR

## Issue
Employee is resigning and needs to know the notice period and offboarding
process, or a manager needs to know what to do when they receive one.

## Resolution
1. Standard notice period is defined in the employee's contract (commonly
   4 weeks, longer for senior roles) — confirm the specific term rather than
   assuming the default.
2. Resignation should be submitted in writing (email is acceptable) to the
   direct manager, with HR copied.
3. Manager notifies HR immediately to trigger the offboarding checklist:
   knowledge transfer plan, access revocation scheduling, and final payroll
   processing.
4. Access revocation is scheduled for the employee's actual last working
   day, not initiated early, unless there's a specific security concern
   flagged by the manager.

## Notes
Any request to waive or shorten notice period requires manager and HR
agreement — this isn't unilaterally decided by either the employee or
manager alone.
