## Symptom
An employee needs a letter confirming their employment, for example for a bank loan,
rental agreement or visa.

## Cause
Employment letters are generated through the HR portal; HR signs them only when a
non-standard format is required.

## Resolution steps
1. The employee requests the letter in the HR portal under Documents, choosing the
   purpose.
2. The standard letter (job title, start date and employment status) is generated at once.
3. Salary is included only if the employee ticks the option to include it.
4. For a format required by an embassy or bank, the employee uploads the template and HR
   completes it within five working days.

## Warnings
HR never confirms employment details to a caller without the employee's written consent.

## Key facts
- Standard employment letters are generated in the HR portal.
- Salary appears on the letter only if the employee chooses to include it.
- Custom-format letters are completed by HR within five working days.
