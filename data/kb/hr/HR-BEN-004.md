## Symptom
An employee wants to increase or decrease the percentage of salary they put into the
retirement plan.

## Cause
Voluntary retirement contributions can be changed at any time, but each change takes
effect only from the next payroll cut-off.

## Resolution steps
1. The employee changes the contribution percentage in the HR portal under Benefits,
   Retirement.
2. Changes made before the payroll cut-off apply to that month; later changes apply from
   the next month.
3. Contributions stay within the plan's minimum and maximum percentages shown in the
   portal.
4. Questions about investment choices go to the plan provider, not HR.

## Warnings
HR does not give investment advice.

## Key facts
- Voluntary retirement contributions can be changed at any time in the HR portal.
- Changes take effect from the next payroll cut-off.
- Investment questions go to the plan provider.
