## Symptom
An employee's corporate credit card is lost or stolen, or shows transactions they did not
make.

## Cause
Corporate cards are issued by the bank; only the bank can block a card.

## Resolution steps
1. The employee calls the bank's 24-hour number on the card programme page at once to
   block the card.
2. They report the loss to the travel and expense team the same day.
3. Unrecognised transactions are disputed with the bank within 30 days of the statement.
4. A replacement card is ordered by the travel and expense team.

## Warnings
Blocking the card is the first step; do not wait for business hours.

## Key facts
- A lost corporate card is blocked by calling the bank's 24-hour number.
- The loss is reported to the travel and expense team the same day.
- Unrecognised transactions are disputed within 30 days of the statement.
