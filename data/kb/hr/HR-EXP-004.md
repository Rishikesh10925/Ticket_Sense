## Symptom
An employee travelling abroad asks how much they can spend on meals per day, or whether
they need receipts for meals.

## Cause
International trips use a per diem: a fixed daily allowance per country that covers meals
and incidentals instead of meal receipts.

## Resolution steps
1. Look up the destination country's per diem rate in the travel policy.
2. Claim the per diem in the expense portal for each travel day; meal receipts are not
   needed.
3. Days with meals provided (for example by a conference) are claimed at a reduced rate.
4. Hotel and transport are claimed separately with receipts.

## Warnings
Per diem and meal receipts cannot both be claimed for the same day.

## Key facts
- International trips use a fixed daily per diem per country.
- Per diem claims do not need meal receipts.
- Days with provided meals are claimed at a reduced rate.
