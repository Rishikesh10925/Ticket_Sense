## Symptom
An employee wants to enrol in or change benefits (health insurance, retirement
contributions) at a time when the portal does not allow it.

## Cause
Benefits can be changed only during the annual open enrolment window, within 30 days of a
qualifying life event, or in a new hire's first 30 days.

## Resolution steps
1. Ask whether a qualifying life event happened (marriage, birth or adoption, loss of
   other coverage) and its exact date.
2. If within 30 days of the event, the employee submits the change in the HR portal with
   proof (for example a marriage or birth certificate).
3. For new hires, check the 30-day window from the start date has not passed.
4. Without a qualifying event, the change waits for the next open enrolment.

## Warnings
The 30 days count from the event date, not from when the employee asks.

## Key facts
- Benefits change only in open enrolment, within 30 days of a qualifying life event, or in a new hire's first 30 days.
- The 30-day window counts from the date of the event.
- Life-event changes need supporting documents.
