## Symptom
An employee asks how to request time off, or whether a request they sent was received.

## Cause
Leave is requested and approved only through the HR portal; emails or chat messages to a
manager do not create a leave record.

## Resolution steps
1. Submit the request in the HR portal under Leave, choosing the leave type and dates.
2. The request goes automatically to the direct manager for approval.
3. Request planned leave at least two weeks ahead where possible.
4. Sick leave can be recorded afterwards, but within three working days of returning.
5. If the manager has not responded within five working days, follow up with them
   directly; silence is not approval.

## Warnings
Leave agreed only by email or chat is not recorded and may be counted as unpaid absence.

## Key facts
- Leave is requested only through the HR portal and approved by the direct manager.
- Planned leave should be requested at least two weeks in advance.
- Sick leave must be recorded within three working days of returning.
- A request without a response is not approved; the employee follows up after five working days.
