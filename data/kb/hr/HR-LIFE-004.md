## Symptom
A former employee asks when they will receive their final pay, or why it has not arrived.

## Cause
Final settlement is paid after exit clearance is complete: equipment returned and sign-off
from the manager and IT. It includes salary up to the last day and payment for unused
annual leave where the country's policy allows it.

## Resolution steps
1. Check whether exit clearance is complete in the HR portal.
2. If equipment has not been returned, give the employee the return instructions.
3. Once clearance is complete, final settlement is paid in the next payroll run.
4. The final payslip and tax documents appear in the leaver's portal access, which stays
   open for 90 days after the last day.

## Warnings
Do not disclose a leaver's settlement details to their former manager.

## Key facts
- Final settlement is paid after exit clearance is complete.
- Unreturned equipment delays exit clearance.
- Leavers keep portal access to payslips for 90 days after their last day.
