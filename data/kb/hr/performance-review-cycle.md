# Performance Review Cycle Overview

**Department:** HR

## Issue
Employee or manager has a question about when reviews happen and how the
process works.

## Resolution
1. Formal performance reviews run on an annual cycle, with a mid-year
   informal check-in.
2. Process: self-assessment submitted first, followed by manager
   assessment, then a calibration step before ratings are finalized and
   shared.
3. New hires within their first 90 days are generally excluded from that
   cycle's formal review and instead get a probation check-in.
4. Compensation decisions tied to the review cycle are communicated
   separately from the performance rating itself, on a different timeline.

## Notes
Employees asking about compensation changes specifically should be
redirected to wait for the separate compensation communication rather than
inferring outcomes from the performance review alone.
