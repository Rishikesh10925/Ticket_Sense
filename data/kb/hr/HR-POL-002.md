## Symptom
An employee or manager asks when reviews happen, how ratings are decided, or when pay
changes are announced.

## Cause
Reviews follow an annual cycle with a mid-year check-in. Ratings are calibrated across
teams before they are shared. Pay decisions are communicated separately.

## Resolution steps
1. Explain the steps: self-assessment, manager assessment, calibration, then the rating is
   shared.
2. New hires in their first 90 days get a probation check-in instead of a formal review.
3. Pay changes are announced in a separate communication after the cycle, on their own
   timeline.
4. Disagreements with a rating are raised with the manager first, then with HR.

## Warnings
Do not predict pay changes from a rating.

## Key facts
- Reviews are annual, with a mid-year check-in.
- Ratings are calibrated before they are shared.
- New hires in their first 90 days get a probation check-in instead.
- Pay changes are communicated separately from ratings.
