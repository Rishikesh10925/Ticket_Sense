## Symptom
An employee asks how many days they must be in the office, or wants an exception to work
remotely for a period.

## Cause
Each role is office-based, hybrid or fully remote, as set in the employment terms. Hybrid
roles have a minimum number of office days per week set by the department.

## Resolution steps
1. Check the employee's role category in their employment terms.
2. For hybrid roles, the department's minimum office days apply.
3. Exceptions (for example temporary full remote for a personal reason) are requested from
   the manager, who decides with HR informed.
4. Equipment for home working is requested through the normal equipment request.

## Warnings
HR clarifies the policy; the manager approves individual exceptions.

## Key facts
- Each role is office-based, hybrid or fully remote under the employment terms.
- Hybrid minimum office days are set by the department.
- Remote-work exceptions are decided by the manager with HR informed.
