## Symptom
An employee cannot find this month's payslip, or asks when salary will be paid.

## Cause
Salary is paid on the last working day of the month and payslips are published two
working days before that. A missing payslip usually means it is not published yet, or the
employee joined or left mid-month and was paid in a separate run.

## Resolution steps
1. Check today's date against the pay date; payslips appear two working days before it.
2. Direct the employee to the HR portal under Payroll, then Payslips.
3. If the pay date has passed and the payslip is missing, check whether the employee was
   in a special run (mid-month starter or leaver) before escalating to payroll.
4. Questions about the amount go to payroll with the specific line item.

## Warnings
Never share payslip or salary details with anyone except the employee.

## Key facts
- Salary is paid on the last working day of the month.
- Payslips are published in the HR portal two working days before the pay date.
- Mid-month starters and leavers may be paid in a separate run.
