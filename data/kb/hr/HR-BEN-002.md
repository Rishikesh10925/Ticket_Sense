## Symptom
An employee asks how to file a health insurance claim, or why one is still not paid.

## Cause
Claims are processed by the insurance carrier, not by HR. In-network providers file
claims themselves; other claims are submitted by the employee in the carrier's portal.

## Resolution steps
1. Ask whether the provider was in-network. If yes, the provider files the claim.
2. For other claims, the employee submits through the carrier's portal with an itemised
   bill and proof of payment.
3. For status, the employee checks the carrier's portal using the claim number.
4. If a claim is beyond the carrier's stated turnaround time, HR escalates it through the
   carrier's employer support line, quoting the claim number only.

## Warnings
Never ask for or record medical details in a ticket; use the claim number only.

## Key facts
- In-network providers file claims directly with the carrier.
- Out-of-network claims are submitted in the carrier's portal, not the HR portal.
- HR escalates overdue claims through the carrier's employer support line.
- Claims are discussed by claim number only, never medical details.
