# Public Holiday Calendar

**Department:** HR

## Issue
Employee wants to know which days are recognized as company holidays this
year.

## Resolution
1. The holiday calendar is published annually on the HR portal and varies
   by country/region office.
2. Employees in a region should reference their specific regional calendar,
   not the headquarters calendar, if they're at a different location.
3. Floating holidays (if offered) are booked the same way as regular leave
   through the leave request process, not automatically applied.
4. Holiday calendar questions for a specific date should be answered by
   checking the current published calendar, since it's revised each year
   and past-year copies are not valid for the current year.

## Notes
Do not confirm a specific date is a holiday from memory — the calendar is
published per year and should always be checked directly.
