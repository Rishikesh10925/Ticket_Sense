## Symptom
An employee asks whether a specific date is a company holiday, or why their calendar
differs from a colleague's in another office.

## Cause
Each country or regional office has its own holiday calendar, published every year in
the HR portal.

## Resolution steps
1. Open the current year's holiday calendar for the employee's office in the HR portal.
2. Check the date there; never answer from memory or an older year's calendar.
3. Explain that colleagues in other offices follow their own calendars.
4. Floating holidays, where offered, are booked like annual leave through the leave
   request.

## Warnings
Last year's calendar is not valid for this year; dates move.

## Key facts
- Holiday calendars are published per year and per office in the HR portal.
- Employees follow their own office's calendar, not headquarters'.
- Floating holidays are booked through the normal leave request.
