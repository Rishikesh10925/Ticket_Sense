## Symptom
An employee asks how to claim a business expense, or why a claim has not been paid.

## Cause
Claims need an itemised receipt and manager approval, and are reimbursed in the payroll
run after approval.

## Resolution steps
1. Submit the claim in the expense portal with an itemised receipt for each item.
2. The manager approves it in the portal.
3. Approved claims are paid with the next salary, not immediately.
4. If a claim is returned for more information, reply in the portal; do not submit a new
   claim.

## Warnings
Category limits (for example meal caps) are policy and cannot be overridden by the
helpdesk; exceptions go to the manager.

## Key facts
- Expense claims need an itemised receipt and manager approval.
- Approved claims are paid with the next salary.
- Returned claims are answered in the portal, not resubmitted.
