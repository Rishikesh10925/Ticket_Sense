## Symptom
An employee wants to resign and asks about the notice period, or a manager has received
a resignation and asks what to do.

## Cause
The notice period is set in each employee's contract; offboarding starts when HR is told.

## Resolution steps
1. Check the notice period in the employee's contract (commonly four weeks, longer for
   senior roles).
2. The employee resigns in writing (email is fine) to their manager, copying HR.
3. The manager informs HR at once so offboarding starts: knowledge transfer, access
   removal on the last day, and final pay.
4. Shortening or waiving the notice period needs agreement from both the manager and HR.

## Warnings
Access is removed on the last working day, not earlier, unless the manager reports a
security concern.

## Key facts
- The notice period is defined in the employee's contract.
- Resignations are submitted in writing to the manager with HR copied.
- Shortening the notice period needs both manager and HR agreement.
- Access is removed on the last working day.
