## Symptom
An employee wants to apply for an open role in another team and asks whether they must
tell their manager.

## Cause
Internal moves follow the internal mobility policy: employees can apply after twelve
months in their current role, and the current manager is informed when the employee is
shortlisted.

## Resolution steps
1. Check the employee has completed twelve months in their current role.
2. The employee applies through the internal jobs page in the HR portal.
3. The current manager is informed by HR only when the employee is shortlisted.
4. If selected, the transfer date is agreed between both managers, normally within four
   weeks.

## Warnings
Do not tell the current manager about an application before shortlisting.

## Key facts
- Employees can apply internally after twelve months in their current role.
- The current manager is informed only when the employee is shortlisted.
- Transfer dates are agreed between both managers, normally within four weeks.
