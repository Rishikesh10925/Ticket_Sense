## Symptom
An employee asks what kinds of leave exist and how many days they are entitled to.

## Cause
Entitlements depend on the employee's country and employment type, so a single number
cannot be given; the categories are the same everywhere.

## Resolution steps
1. Explain the categories: annual leave (accrued monthly), sick leave (from the first day
   of employment), casual leave (short-notice personal leave, limited days per year), and
   separate policies for bereavement, parental and jury duty leave.
2. Point the employee to the leave policy page for their own country in the HR portal for
   exact days.
3. Unused annual leave carries over to the next year only up to the cap on that page.
4. Tell the employee their current balance is shown on the Leave page of the HR portal.

## Warnings
Do not quote day counts from another country's policy.

## Key facts
- Annual leave accrues monthly and carries over only up to a cap.
- Sick leave is available from the first day of employment.
- Exact entitlements depend on country and are on the country's policy page.
- A doctor's note is needed for sick absences longer than three consecutive days.
