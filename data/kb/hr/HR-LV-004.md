## Symptom
An employee who is expecting or adopting a child asks how to arrange parental leave.

## Cause
Parental leave has its own policy with notice and documentation requirements, separate
from annual leave.

## Resolution steps
1. Ask the employee to notify their manager and HR at least eight weeks before the planned
   start date.
2. They submit the parental leave request in the HR portal under Leave, choosing the
   parental leave type.
3. They upload the supporting document (medical certificate of expected due date, or
   adoption placement document).
4. HR confirms the entitlement for their country and the return date in writing.

## Warnings
Do not ask for medical details beyond the expected due date.

## Key facts
- Parental leave notice is due at least eight weeks before the start date.
- The request is submitted in the HR portal with a supporting document.
- HR confirms the entitlement and return date in writing.
