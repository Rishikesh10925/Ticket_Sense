## Symptom
An employee received or was offered a gift, meal or event ticket from a supplier or
customer and asks whether they may accept it.

## Cause
The code of conduct limits gifts to avoid conflicts of interest. Small gifts are allowed;
larger ones must be declared or refused.

## Resolution steps
1. Ask for the estimated value and who offered it.
2. Gifts worth up to the policy limit (50 in local currency) may be accepted without
   declaration.
3. Anything above the limit must be declared in the HR portal's gift register before
   accepting, and the manager approves or declines.
4. Cash, vouchers and gifts during an active tender are always refused.

## Warnings
Gifts from a supplier in an active tender must be refused regardless of value.

## Key facts
- Gifts up to 50 in local currency may be accepted without declaration.
- Gifts above the limit are declared in the gift register and approved by the manager.
- Cash, vouchers and gifts during an active tender are always refused.
