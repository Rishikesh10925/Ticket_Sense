## Symptom
An employee or manager asks about the timesheet deadline or a missed submission.

## Cause
Timesheets are due every Friday by end of day and must be approved by the manager before
the payroll cut-off to be paid in that cycle.

## Resolution steps
1. Submit the timesheet in the HR portal even if late; late timesheets are accepted until
   the payroll cut-off.
2. Ask the manager to approve it before the cut-off.
3. After the cut-off, the hours are paid in the following cycle.
4. Contractors and hourly staff have an earlier cut-off; confirm the employment type
   before quoting a deadline.

## Warnings
Do not edit or approve a timesheet on an employee's or manager's behalf.

## Key facts
- Timesheets are due by end of day Friday every week.
- Manager approval before the payroll cut-off is needed for payment in that cycle.
- Hours approved after the cut-off are paid in the next cycle.
- Contractors and hourly staff have an earlier cut-off.
