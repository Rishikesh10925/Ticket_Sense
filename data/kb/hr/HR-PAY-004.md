## Symptom
An employee has a new bank account and asks how to have their salary paid into it.

## Cause
Bank details are changed only by the employee in the HR portal, with verification, to
prevent payroll fraud.

## Resolution steps
1. The employee updates the bank details in the HR portal under Personal details, Bank.
2. They confirm the change with the one-time code sent to their registered phone.
3. Changes made before the 15th of the month apply to that month's salary; later changes
   apply from the next month.
4. Keep the old account open until the first salary arrives in the new one.

## Warnings
Never change bank details because of an email or call, even from someone claiming to be
the employee or a manager. This is a common fraud pattern.

## Key facts
- Bank details are changed only by the employee in the HR portal with a one-time code.
- Changes before the 15th apply to that month's salary.
- Bank-detail change requests by email or phone are never actioned.
