## Symptom
An employee says their salary this month is lower or higher than expected, or a deduction
on the payslip is unfamiliar.

## Cause
Usually a change that took effect this month: a tax change, a benefit deduction, unpaid
leave, a mid-month salary change, or an arrears payment from last month.

## Resolution steps
1. Ask the employee for the payslip month and the specific line that looks wrong.
2. Compare it with the previous month's payslip to find the line that changed.
3. Check for unpaid leave, benefit enrolments or salary changes that took effect this month.
4. If the cause is not clear, raise a payroll query with the line item and both payslips.
5. Confirmed errors are corrected in the next payroll run unless payroll agrees an
   off-cycle payment.

## Warnings
Do not promise a correction or an off-cycle payment; payroll decides.

## Key facts
- Comparing with the previous payslip shows which line changed.
- Payroll queries include the payslip month and the specific line item.
- Confirmed errors are corrected in the next payroll run unless payroll approves an off-cycle payment.
