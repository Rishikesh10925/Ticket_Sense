## Symptom
A manager or new hire reports that accounts, laptop or access are not ready, or asks what
should happen before the first day.

## Cause
IT provisions accounts and equipment from the role profile the manager submits. When the
role profile is submitted late, day-one readiness slips.

## Resolution steps
1. Check when the manager submitted the role profile. It should go in as soon as the
   offer is signed.
2. If it is missing, ask the manager to submit it now and mark the request urgent.
3. Confirm HR sent pre-start paperwork (contract, tax forms, bank details) to the new hire.
4. On day one, the new hire collects a badge at reception with photo ID and completes
   mandatory compliance training within the first week.

## Warnings
Do not let a new hire use another employee's account while theirs is being created.

## Key facts
- IT provisions accounts and equipment from the manager's role profile.
- The role profile should be submitted as soon as the offer is signed.
- Badges are collected at reception with photo ID on day one.
- Mandatory compliance training is completed within the first week.
