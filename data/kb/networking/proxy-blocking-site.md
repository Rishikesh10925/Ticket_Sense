# Proxy Blocking a Required Website

**Department:** Networking

## Issue
User cannot reach a specific website needed for work; browser shows a proxy
block page or a generic connection error while other sites work fine.

## Resolution
1. Confirm the exact URL/domain being blocked and the business reason it's
   needed.
2. Check the proxy's category-based blocking rules — most blocks are by
   content category (e.g., "file sharing," "uncategorized") rather than a
   specific deliberate block on that domain.
3. Submit a proxy allowlist request with the domain and business
   justification; this goes through the standard change process, not a
   direct edit.
4. For a domain that's miscategorized (e.g., a legitimate business tool
   flagged as "uncategorized"), note this explicitly so it can be
   recategorized rather than just allowlisted as an exception.

## Notes
Do not disable the proxy or route around it locally to "fix" access —
allowlist the specific domain through the proper process instead.
