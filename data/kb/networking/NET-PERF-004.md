## Symptom
Many users on the same floor or office report that websites and cloud apps are slow at
the same time.

## Cause
A shared link or switch is saturated: a large download or backup, a faulty uplink running
at reduced speed, or the office internet link reaching capacity.

## Resolution steps
1. Confirm that several users in the same area are affected, and record when it started.
2. Ask whether anything unusual is running: large software deployments, backups or
   video events.
3. Escalate to network engineering with the location, the time and the number of users;
   they check link utilisation and uplink errors.
4. Keep the ticket open as a parent for the other users' reports.

## Warnings
Do not restart network equipment yourself.

## Key facts
- Many users slow at once in one area points to a saturated shared link or uplink.
- Network engineering checks link utilisation and uplink errors.
- Related user reports are linked to one parent ticket.
