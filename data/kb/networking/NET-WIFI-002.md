## Symptom
An employee asks for internet access for a visitor, contractor or vendor who is on site.

## Cause
Visitors may not join the corporate network. Guest access is a separate, isolated network
with time-limited credentials sponsored by an employee.

## Resolution steps
1. Confirm the request comes from an employee who sponsors the guest. Visitors cannot
   request access for themselves.
2. Create a guest credential or day pass in the guest portal, with an expiry that matches
   the visit. Never issue a credential without an expiry.
3. Record the sponsor and the visit dates in the ticket for audit.
4. If the visitor needs internal systems, explain that this is a separate access request
   with its own approval. Never put a visitor on the corporate network.

## Warnings
Guest Wi-Fi is deliberately bandwidth-limited and isolated; "slow" guest Wi-Fi is not a
fault to fix.

## Key facts
- Guest access must be sponsored by an employee.
- Guest credentials always expire, matching the visit.
- The guest network is isolated from internal systems by design.
