## Symptom
Other people in the room are connected, but the corporate network name does not appear
in the user's list of available Wi-Fi networks.

## Cause
The corporate network runs only on the 5 GHz band. An outdated driver, an adapter set to
2.4 GHz only, or airplane mode or a hardware Wi-Fi switch hides it.

## Resolution steps
1. Check that airplane mode is off and the Wi-Fi toggle (or keyboard key) is on.
2. Ask whether other 5 GHz networks are visible. If only 2.4 GHz networks show, check the
   adapter's band setting and set it to auto or 5 GHz preferred.
3. Update the wireless driver from the software centre and restart.
4. If it is still missing, the adapter may be faulty: raise a hardware ticket.

## Warnings
Do not share a phone hotspot with corporate devices as a long-term workaround.

## Key facts
- The corporate Wi-Fi network is broadcast only on 5 GHz.
- An adapter limited to 2.4 GHz or an old driver hides the corporate network.
- Airplane mode or a hardware Wi-Fi switch hides all networks.
