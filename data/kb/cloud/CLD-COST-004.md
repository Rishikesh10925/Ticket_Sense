## Symptom
A project owner receives an email that their cloud budget has reached a threshold, for
example 80 percent of the monthly budget, and asks what to do.

## Cause
Each project has a monthly budget with alert thresholds. Alerts can be on actual spend or
on forecast spend. Budget alerts only notify; they do not stop resources.

## Resolution steps
1. Check whether the alert is for actual or forecast spend.
2. Compare the current spend with the same point last month to see whether it is unusual.
3. If it is expected growth, the project owner asks finance for a budget change.
4. If it is unexpected, follow the cost anomaly investigation with the owning team.

## Warnings
Do not shut down a project's resources because of a budget alert; the owner decides.

## Key facts
- Budget alerts can be on actual or on forecast spend.
- Budget alerts only notify; they do not stop resources.
- Budget changes are requested from finance by the project owner.
